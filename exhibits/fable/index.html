Phase 00 · the low

Tech meets technical.

This page is one full cycle. You're standing at the trough. Everything on it — the field behind this text, the dial in the corner, the sound if you turn it on — runs off a single phase variable: how far you've scrolled toward the next low.

Phase 01 · advance

Energy builds on the way up.

Watch the field. It was nearly still at the top of the page — sparse, dim, compressed, the way markets feel at a low nobody trusts yet. As you descend it warms, brightens, and starts to move, because its turbulence and color are mapped to your position in the page-cycle. Maximum energy arrives at the crest, one section down. That's the whole design argument here: flash that isn't wired to meaning is just heat. Every loud thing on this page is a phase readout.

The vocabulary streaming past below is the working language of the research — cycle counts measured trough to trough, translation as the skew of crests, anchors and invalidations as the honesty mechanism. Methodology, not picks.

Phase 03 · decline

What cools down stays true.

The field is losing energy behind you now. What survives the decline is the part worth keeping — the rules the flash was wired to.

Every effect is a readout. The shader, the dial, the drone, and the chart all express one variable. Nothing glows for free.

No invented prices. The series is synthetic and says so on its face. Concept pages don't get to look like research.

The model interprets; it never decides. Language models narrate validated state. No authority over data, signals, or thresholds.

Claim only what runs. This page renders WebGL2 with a canvas fallback. It does not mention capabilities it doesn't ship.

Self-contained or nothing. One file, no frameworks, no runtime CDN for code. If the fonts fail, the system stack holds.
